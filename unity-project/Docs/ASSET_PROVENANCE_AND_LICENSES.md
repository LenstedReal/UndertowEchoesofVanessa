# Asset Provenance & Licensing Ledger — Undertow: Echoes of Vanessa

The OBJ meshes and PBR maps in this archive are locally generated procedural assets for the Undertow project. No third-party marketplace packs or intentionally imported copyrighted image samples are included in these generated files. The project owner must retain source files and assign commercial distribution rights explicitly before release; this ledger does not grant MIT, CC0 or another open-source license.

## 1. Procedural 3D meshes

`Assets/_Project/Art/Meshes/` contains ten original procedural OBJ meshes: antique brass lantern, ship bell, surgeon's hook, Vanessa music box, galleon hull/deck, mast/sails, starboard ring, clock dial, temple column/mirror and pale leviathan whale. The meshes have authored geometry and UV coordinates, but remain prototype-to-mid-complexity assets. Production release still requires artist review, bespoke hero detailing, material breakup, damage/fasteners/ropes, topology review and appropriate collision LODs.

## 2. Procedural PBR texture sets

`Assets/_Project/Textures/` contains six 4096×4096 PBR sets (Albedo, tangent-space Normal and HDRP MaskMap): wet aged deck wood, antique brass, torn black sail, sunken temple stone, storm ocean and underwater water. MaskMap channel packing is R=Metallic, G=Ambient Occlusion, B=Detail, A=Smoothness. Import settings request HQ compression, mipmaps and streaming mipmaps. Inspect all maps in the Unity HDRP material inspector before commercial release; source resolution does not guarantee photorealistic appearance or runtime performance.

## 3. Audio and animation

The project includes procedural bell-tone synthesis, procedural ambience/audio fallback, first-person lantern sway, sail movement, floating debris, storm flashes, underwater particles and leviathan swimming motion. It does not include recorded performances for Vanessa, Mimic or Ruth, final music, a completed licensed environmental sound package, FMOD banks, facial animation or humanoid NPC animation. Record or license release audio and document each license before Steam publication.
